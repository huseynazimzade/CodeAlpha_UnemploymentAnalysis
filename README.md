\## Task 2: Unemployment Analysis with Python



The goal of this project is to analyze unemployment rate data in India

and investigate the impact of Covid-19 on unemployment.



\### Dataset

\- Unemployment rate data (2019-2020)

\- Columns: Region, Date, Estimated Unemployment Rate, Estimated Employed,

&#x20; Estimated Labour Participation Rate



\### Approach

\- Cleaned the data (removed nulls, converted date column)

\- Explored unemployment trends over time

\- Visualized average unemployment by region

\- Investigated the impact of Covid-19 (before vs after March 2020)

\- Identified monthly trends and patterns



\### Results

\- Covid-19 caused a significant increase in unemployment after March 2020

\- Urban regions were more affected than rural areas

\- Unemployment peaked in April-May 2020, then gradually declined
